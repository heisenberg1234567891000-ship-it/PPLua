Homelander - fan mod for PPLua (People Playground). Unofficial; not affiliated with The Boys, Amazon,
Sony Pictures Television or Studio Minus. All bundled art and sounds were made from scratch for this mod
(by tools/make_homelander_assets.py in the PPLua repository).

Spawn: category "Supes" (the "+" menu of extra categories), item "Homelander".

Keys (work for every Homelander on the map):
  H (hold)  heat vision at the mouse cursor - burns, cuts limbs off, melts small objects
  J         take off / land
  R (hold)  fly toward the cursor (takes off by itself)
  N         speed punch toward the cursor - crushes what he hits
  B         thunder clap - shockwave toward the cursor
Right-click his chest for "Fly / land" and "Thunder clap" buttons too.

Heat vision sound: the mod uses its own synthesized loop (sounds/laser.wav). To use a sound of your own, put it
at sounds/laser.mp3. A clip of 2 seconds or less simply loops while H is held. A longer clip is played in parts:
it starts at 1.35 s, repeats 2.0-3.3 s while H is held, and jumps to 3.95 s for the tail when you let go, so
lay out your clip that way. Hitting people sounds like searing flesh; other materials use their own sound.

Your own look (only files you have the right to use):
  custom/skin.png  a full People Playground human skin texture - replaces the painted suit
  custom/cape.png  the cape sprite, hanging from its top edge

Always on: bulletproof, fireproof, immune to explosions, cannot be cut, crushed or dismembered,
regenerates twice a second (even a stopped heart).
